## Redactions
Four table rows in `reviews/PREREG__review_4.md`, `PREREG__review_5.md` and `PREREG__review_9.md` are redacted in this public copy, and each is marked in place. They recorded that decisions about **other, unrelated manuscripts** by the same authors do not conflict with this plan. Nothing about this plan, its analyses or its decision rules is redacted. The unredacted reviews are kept by the authors and available to the editor on request.
